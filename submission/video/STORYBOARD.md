# Teampot film: storyboard

**Length:** about 1:50, cut to the music.

**Format:** 1920×1080, 60 fps. Rendered at 120 fps and blended frame-pairs for natural motion blur.

**Music:** "background music" by sigmamusicart (Pixabay license: commercial use, no credit required). 108 BPM; one bar is 2.18 s; a phrase is 4 bars (8.7 s). Swappable; alternates are in Downloads (Mixkit, Bensound). The Catalyst track is used only for a comparison mix, not for publishing.

**Sound effects:** only the Fomo pack (`Downloads/daybreak pitch deck/Fomo_video_sound_effects_extracted/cleaned_sfx`), one sound family throughout.

## Rules
- **Type:** Geist at weight 200–300, large and thin, with tight tracking. Geist Mono for small capital labels. Never heavy display type.
- **Backgrounds:** black (#0A0A0B) and off-white (#F5F5F5) alternate. Text is the opposite colour. Clay (#E8552D) only for the underline and the logo lid. Light beams use the department colours (orange, blue, green); red only for the refusal.
- **Motion:** everything is a function of time, so renders are exact. Words rise in from a soft blur. The camera moves are pushes, pans and one zoom into a letter. Phones enter from the side with a 3D swing.
- **Sound:** a sound on every event, never stacked on top of a music hit.
