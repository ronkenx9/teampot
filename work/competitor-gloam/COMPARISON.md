## Comparative judgment

- Functionality: split. Teampot's testnet departments, controlled spends, approvals and payroll form a coherent business workflow; Gloam has private sends/payroll and selective proofs in source. Neither is proven production-ready by this comparison.
- Impact: slight Gloam edge in potential reusable infrastructure, not measured market size; either could support a substantial business.
- Novelty: Gloam edge from private payroll plus selectively verifiable totals/payments. Teampot's defensible distinction is delegated budgets with network-enforced caps/vendor lists, beyond generic onchain payroll.
- UX: Teampot edge for ordinary company employees and Finance; Gloam requires wallet/private-balance/backup concepts. Public entry inspected; no Gloam financial flow executed.
- Open source/composition: Gloam edge via contracts, circuits, SDK, MCP and payment integrations. SDK/contract MIT licensing confirmed; do not claim uniform root license. Teampot public MIT app and integration evidence are strengths.
- Business plan: Teampot clearer initial buyer/workflow and SaaS/payout pricing proposal; no validated monetization winner. Gloam fees/API/payroll model is proposed and demand metrics don't prove revenue.
- Tempo relevance (additional interpretation, not seventh official criterion): Teampot strong through treasury→department→head→employee access-key hierarchy. Gloam addresses confidentiality and includes Tempo issuer-policy/passkey support; multichain design does not disqualify it.
