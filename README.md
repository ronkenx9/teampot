# Teampot

**Work money, finally fun.** Payday, team pots and contractor pay for everyone a company works with, built on [Tempo](https://tempo.xyz).

**Live demo:** https://teampot.vercel.app (Tempo testnet, test money). Use **View as** to switch between Finance, a team lead, an employee and a contractor.

- **Payday** — everyone paid in one transaction, in about a second.
- **Team pots** — each person spends with their own key: a monthly limit and an approved-vendor list, both enforced by the chain.
- **Held for approval** — a payment to a new vendor or over the limit is stopped by the chain and waits for the lead's one-tap OK.
- **Contractors** — send an invoice, get paid the moment it's approved.
- **Face ID** — turn it on and your device passkey becomes your spending key. No wallets, seed phrases or gas.
