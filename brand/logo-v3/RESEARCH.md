# Teampot Logo v3 Research

## 1. Category Audit

### How nearby companies mark themselves

- **Ramp**: mostly a restrained finance-ops wordmark system, with official press materials emphasizing modern software, time and money savings, and media assets rather than a pictorial mascot. The category signal is sober, efficient and B2B, not expressive. Source: [Ramp press](https://ramp.com/press).
- **Brex**: heavy custom wordmark, historically supported by a flag idea. Brex explains the flag as a symbol of ambition, reinvention and "winds of change"; the mark is more metaphor than literal money object. Sources: [Brex press links](https://www.brex.com/journal/press), [Brex rebrand note](https://www.brex.com/journal/rebranding-brex).
- **Mercury**: startup banking competitor with a sparse, dark wordmark plus an orbital/monogram-like symbol in many placements. The useful lesson is restraint: the mark does not show cash, cards or banking hardware directly.
- **Rippling**: bold uppercase wordmark and geometric "R" monogram in a plum/dark palette. Its press page packages a full logo suite for light/dark usage, and the company now frames itself as one connected HR, IT and Finance platform. Source: [Rippling press](https://www.rippling.com/company/press).
- **Deel**: wordmark-led global-work brand with official logos and product screenshots distributed through a press kit. The identity leans friendly/global but not diagrammatic. Source: [Deel press](https://www.deel.com/press/).
- **Gusto**: explicitly rejects needing an abstract symbol most of the time. Its guidelines say the name is ownable, the lowercase "g" carries a subtle smile, and the symbol is reserved for small-use cases like favicons. Source: [Gusto brand guidelines PDF](https://prod.gusto-assets.com/media/Gusto-brand-guidelines.pdf).
- **Linear**: monochrome-first wordmark, concise logomark for tight layouts, and a highly controlled icon. Linear says the wordmark has stronger recognition and reserves its subtle blue mostly for backgrounds. Source: [Linear brand guidelines](https://linear.app/brand?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-text-block).
- **Notion**: black and white cube logo plus wordmark, deliberately simple and system-like. Notion maintains public brand guidelines and media kit assets. Sources: [Notion media kit](https://notion.notion.site/Media-Kit-205535b1d9c4440497a3d7a2ac096286), [Notion trademark guidelines](https://www.notion.so/Notion-Trademark-Usage-Guidelines-9826313c686a4f6e9d8a48347162714b).

### Category cliches to avoid

- Upward arrows, growth chevrons, "savings" sparks.
- Coins, piggy banks, wallets, cards, bank buildings.
- Shields, locks, generic keys-as-security.
- Globes, pin maps, people-in-a-circle for global teams.
- Org-chart boxes, trees, honeycombs, hub-and-spoke diagrams.
- Pie charts, progress rings, dashboard gauges.
- Generic blue, bank green, fintech gradients.
- Mascots, faces, cute pots, teapots, steam.

## 3. Three Concepts To Build

### A. Balanced Mobile

One-line idea: independent team budgets hang from one balanced company mechanism.

Why it made the cut: it is the most distinctive and most "decentralised grown-up" concept. It has a real motion idea built in: each arm tilts as team budgets move, while the whole system returns to balance.

Primary risk: it is more elegant than immediately financial, and needs heavier geometry to survive at 16 px.

### B. Rooms

One-line idea: every team gets its own room inside one financial house.

Why it made the cut: it is the clearest expression of "Finance sets the frame, departments decide." It also inherits the existing Fill-gauge logic without looking like a dashboard chart.

Primary risk: if the rooms become too regular, it starts to look like a generic app grid or Notion block.

### C. Key Ring

One-line idea: every team holds its own spending key, all kept on one ring.

Why it made the cut: it is the most Tempo-native metaphor because access keys are the product mechanism. It is simple, app-icon friendly, and can animate as keys are issued, revoked, or used.

Primary risk: keys can suggest security software before budget autonomy, so the geometry must feel like permissions for spending, not a lock brand.
