# Teampot Logo v3 Recommendation

## Concepts

1. **Balanced Mobile**: independent team budgets hang from one balanced company mechanism.
   - Best quality: distinctive, calm, decentralised, not fintech-default.
   - Risk: too delicate at 16 px, and slightly too art-world unless the rest of the brand system supports it.

2. **Rooms**: every team gets its own room inside one financial house.
   - Best quality: clearest expression of "Finance sets the frame, departments decide."
   - Risk: if over-regularized, it can drift into generic app-grid or Notion-block territory.

3. **Key Ring**: every team holds its own spending key, all kept on one ring.
   - Best quality: the most Tempo-native metaphor because access keys are the actual mechanism.
   - Risk: it may read as security software before it reads as team budget autonomy.

## Recommendation

Pick **B. Rooms**.

It is the strongest Teampot mark because it explains the product without showing money: one frame, independent rooms, live budget state. It also survives the 16/32/64 px checks better than Balanced Mobile and feels less generic-security than Key Ring. The mark has enough product truth to become more than a logo: every pot can inherit the same room-fill behavior in the app.

The honest risk is that Rooms needs disciplined spacing and asymmetry. If it becomes a perfect 3 by 3 grid, it loses the "departments run themselves" idea and becomes a generic SaaS tile. Keep the irregular room sizes and the negative corridors.

## Motion Idea

The company frame stays fixed while each room fills, drains, or changes Clay state according to its team's budget. On a spend or approval, a small block travels through the negative corridor and lands in the target room, then the whole plan settles back into stillness. For payday or quarter close, all rooms pulse once in sequence from left to right, showing one company made of independent budgets.
