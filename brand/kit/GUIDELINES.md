# Teampot — Brand Guidelines v1

**Idea:** the T-Pot. The rim is a T, the bowl holds the team's money, a coin drops in. Teampot ≈ teapot: warm, a little playful, never crypto-looking.
**Line:** Budgets your team actually wants to save.

## Logo
- Lockups: horizontal (default), stacked, symbol only. Files in this folder; web icons in `exports/`.
- Clear space: the coin's diameter (44u on the 256 grid) on every side.
- Minimum size: symbol 16 px; horizontal lockup 96 px wide.
- Backgrounds: colour on Paper/white; reversed (white + clay o) on Ink; white + ink bowl on Clay.
- Don't: recolour the bowl outside the palette, add gradients/shadows to the master, stretch, rotate, or set the wordmark in another font.

## Colour
| Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Clay | #E8552D | Signature: the pot, CTAs, big numbers. Large text/graphics only on light backgrounds. |
| Clay Deep | #B8401C | Small clay text on light backgrounds (accessible). |
| Ink | #141414 | Text, rim, coin. |
| Paper | #F6F4F0 | Default background. |
| Sand | #F1E4D8 | Surfaces, pills, cards. |

## Type
Inter Tight (Open Font License). ExtraBold Italic: wordmark and money figures. Medium: headlines, tight tracking (-0.035em). Regular: body and UI.

## Voice (non-crypto-native)
Say dollars, Face ID, pay link, receipt, held for approval, earned while unspent. Never: wallet, token, gas, chain, stablecoin in the product UI.

## Source
Everything is generated by `../gen-kit.mjs` (Node + opentype.js). Wordmark outlined from Inter Tight 800 Italic.
