# Teampot Brand Kit v4

## Positioning
- Headline: "Every team runs its own money."
- Supporting line: "Finance sets the frame. Departments decide. Everyone's paid and spends from their phone, with limits the network enforces."
- Problem: every money decision in a company gets choked up to one admin desk.

## Assets
- Use the locked logo files from `brand/logo-v4/final/`.
- Primary logo: `teampot-logo.svg`.
- Reversed logo: `teampot-logo-reversed.svg`.
- Symbol and app icons: use the matching symbol/app-icon files in the same folder.

## Color
- Clay `#E8552D`: the only accent color.
- Ink `#141414`: primary text and dark surfaces.
- Paper `#F6F4F0`: page background.
- Neutral `#6F6A63`: secondary text.

## Type
- Use Inter Tight.
- Headlines, product money, and major numbers use ExtraBold Italic.
- Letter spacing stays at `0`.

## UI Mark
- The 4 by 4 Fill block is a budget-left gauge, not the logo.
- Use it inside product surfaces only: department funding, member room, perks, and approval states.

## Product Screens
- Use real Teampot app surfaces: Finance overview, department head approvals/settings, and phone spend.
- Keep app screens calm and functional: no fake testimonials, fake metrics, emoji feature icons, or extra accent colors.

- `board.html` is the source board.
